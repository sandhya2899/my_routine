The Golden Kitchen Rule: At 6:30 PM, simply toss your pre-boiled legumes/protein & chopped veggies into the pan to simmer on low heat. By the time you finish your shower at 7:45 PM, a steaming hot home meal is ready!

Buffer Rules & Guilt-Free Flow

Protections to eliminate routine burnout

Guilt-Free Zone
1

The Morning Cushion (9:15 AM - 9:30 AM)

A dedicated 15-minute buffer before stepping out for work prevents morning rush anxiety and sets a calm tone for the day.

2

The Cook-First Sequence (6:30 PM - 7:45 PM)

Entering the kitchen right after the gym turns meal cooking into a low-effort step. Showering *after* cooking guarantees you sit down to dinner feeling clean and relaxed.

3

The Spillover Cushion (9:45 PM Shift)

If a job application or interview prep extends past 9:45 PM, shift your 30-minute room cleaning to the alternate day or weekend without breaking your schedule.
